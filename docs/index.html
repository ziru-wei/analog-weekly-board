Browser extension for Chrome and Firefox

Analog Weekly Board

A tactile corkboard for the week. Pin notes, photos and links; when Sunday ends, the board archives itself and a clean one begins.

View on GitHubPrivacy

What it does

Weekly boardsMonday to Sunday, one board per week. The first week may be partial.
Automatic archiveFinished weeks collect on the Dashboard — double-click outside the board to open it.
Photos, notes, linksPaste an image or a URL. Frame, enlarge and crop photos in place.
Optional syncSign in with Google to sync across devices through a hidden folder in your own Drive.

Google sign-in

Sign-in is optional and used only for syncing. The app asks for access to its own hidden app-data folder in Google Drive — it cannot see any of your other files — and your basic profile (name, email, photo) to show who is connected. Details in the Privacy Policy.
