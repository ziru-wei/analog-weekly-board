Analog Weekly Board

A tactile weekly corkboard for your browser.

Pin notes, photos and links to a corkboard. Each Monday–Sunday week gets its own board; when the week ends it is archived automatically and a fresh board begins. Everything is stored in your browser, and you can optionally sign in with Google to sync your boards across devices through a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive.

Get it

  • Chrome and Firefox extensions and the source code: github.com/ziru-wei/analog-weekly-board

Google sign-in

Sign-in is optional and used only for syncing. The app requests access to its own hidden app-data folder in Google Drive (it cannot see any of your other Drive files) and your basic profile (name, email, photo) to show who is signed in. See the Privacy Policy.
